<objective>
Close the Phase 7 UAT gap by making the category editor modal easier to scan and use: split the current mixed form/position/destructive-action flow into clear internal sections while preserving every category mutation and close-guard behavior from 07-02.

Purpose: Address the UAT issue "Надо разбить модалку на подкатегории" without expanding Phase 7 into quick category reassignment or broader admin redesign.
Output: Sectioned category modal UI, scoped styles and focused category modal regression tests.
</objective>
